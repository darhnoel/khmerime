KhmerIME

Privacy Policy

KhmerIME · Last updated 1 August 2026

KhmerIME does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

KhmerIME is a Khmer language keyboard that converts romanized input into Khmer script. It works entirely on your device and has no server component.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, KhmerIME does not collect:

  • What you type, in any application
  • Contact information, names, or email addresses
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics

Network access

KhmerIME makes no network requests. Transliteration, candidate ranking, and the on-device language model all run locally. The app functions identically with no internet connection, because it never uses one.

Full Access

On iOS, keyboards can request a permission called Full Access, which would allow a keyboard to send what you type off the device. KhmerIME does not request Full Access and does not need it for any feature. Without it, the keyboard extension is technically unable to transmit your keystrokes anywhere.

Data stored on your device

KhmerIME keeps a small typing history locally so that words you commit are ranked higher next time. This stays in the app's private storage on your device, is never uploaded, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

KhmerIME contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Children

KhmerIME is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Open source

KhmerIME's engine and keyboard are open source. You can verify every claim on this page by reading the code at github.com/darhnoel/khmerime.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date. Any change that involved collecting data would also require a new app release and an updated privacy disclosure on the App Store.
